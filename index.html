Approach

"We treat a listing the way an architect treats a site — read the light, the bones, the neighbours — before we ever price it."

Plinth was founded on a simple objection to how homes are usually sold: wide-angle lenses, adjectives doing the work facts should, and a process built for volume rather than fit.

Instead, every home we represent is documented like a drawing set — true dimensions, real light studies, and a description of what it's actually like to live there, not just what it looks like from the curb.

— Marguerite Sorel, Founding Broker

How it works

Three stages. No detours.

01 / Consult

Site & brief

We walk the property together, talk through timeline and priorities, and put a real number on it — not an anchor price to negotiate down from.

02 / Curate

Document & list

Measured drawings, light studies, and copy that describes the house honestly. It goes live once it's ready, not before.

03 / Close

Negotiate & close
